Date of Judgment: 15th March, 2024

1. This application has been filed by the petitioner seeking regular bail under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 in connection with FIR No. 45 of 2023, registered at Police Station Exampleganj for offences punishable under Sections 420 and 406 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860.

2. The prosecution alleges that the petitioner accepted a sum of Rs. 5,00,000 from the complainant on 10.01.2023 on the promise of supplying goods and thereafter failed to deliver the goods or return the money. The petitioner was arrested on 02.02.2023 and has remained in judicial custody since then.

3. The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that the dispute is essentially of a civil nature, that the petitioner has no criminal antecedents, and that the charge-sheet has already been filed so custodial interrogation is no longer required. The learned counsel for the State opposed the application and submitted that the amount involved is substantial.

4. The question for consideration is whether the petitioner is entitled to be released on bail pending trial.

5. I have considered the submissions and perused the record. The investigation is complete and the charge-sheet has been filed. The offences alleged are triable by a Magistrate and the maximum punishment is not such as to justify continued detention of the petitioner. The petitioner has been in custody for more than one year and the trial is not likely to conclude soon. There is no material to show that the petitioner is likely to flee from justice or tamper with evidence.

6. Having regard to these circumstances, this Court finds that the petitioner has made out a case for the grant of bail. Nothing stated in this order shall be treated as an expression on the merits of the case.

7. The Bail Application is accordingly allowed. The petitioner shall be released on bail on furnishing a personal bond of Rs. 50,000 with one surety of the like amount to the satisfaction of the trial court. The petitioner shall appear before the trial court on every date of hearing. Pending applications, if any, stand disposed of.
